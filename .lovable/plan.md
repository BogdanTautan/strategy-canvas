

## Model Lifecycle Framework — Single Page App

### Layout
- **Left Sidebar** with the Shadcn Sidebar component, styled to match the reference site (clean white/light gray, minimal design)
- **Main Content Area** on the right showing tiles/cards based on the selected menu item

### Left Sidebar Menu
Three clickable menu items, styled similarly to the reference (with a count badge showing "3" for each):
1. **1. Model Strategy and Planning** (3)
2. **2. Data Management** (3)
3. **5. Model Approval & Governance** (3)

The first item is selected by default. Clicking a menu item highlights it and swaps the content on the right.

### Main Content Area
When a menu item is selected, the right side shows:
- A **phase title** (e.g. "Model Strategy and Planning") with subtitle "3 topics to explore"
- A **3-column grid of cards/tiles**, each with:
  - A bold title
  - Empty body (placeholder space maintained for future content)
  - Similar card styling to the reference site (white cards, subtle border/shadow, consistent height)

#### Card content per section:
- **Model Strategy and Planning**: Business Objective Definition · Use Case Assessment · Regulatory & Ethical Considerations
- **Data Management**: Data Sourcing · Data Quality · Data Governance
- **Model Approval & Governance**: Model Review · Model Approval · Model Inventory

### Design & Styling
- Clean, professional look matching the reference site's feel
- Light background, white cards with subtle borders
- Blue/indigo accent for the active sidebar item (similar to reference)
- Responsive layout — sidebar collapses on mobile
- No top horizontal tab bar (simplified from reference)
